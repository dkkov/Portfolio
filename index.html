A service business answers the same twenty questions all day (prices, schedule, policies) and loses everyone who asks after hours. I built a web chat assistant grounded in the school's real pricing and rules. It answers, books a free trial by collecting and validating the lead, saves it to a database and pings staff on Telegram.

It remembers the conversation and checks every reply before it sends, so it never invents a price. No LangChain: I hand-built the routing, memory and tool-calling. Open it and talk to it yourself.
